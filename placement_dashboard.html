Key findings

  • Branch matters most for salary: CSE earns about 2.2x Civil (12.55 vs 5.73 LPA).
  • Internships are a big lever: 0 internships gives 49% placed; 2 internships gives 90%.
  • CGPA still counts: chance climbs from 41% (below 6) to 93% (above 9).
  • Two firms dominate: Accenture and TCS give about 59% of all placements.
  • Skills: SQL and web skills (React, HTML) beat MATLAB by about 18 points.

Recommendation

Build 1-2 internships and solid projects early, keep CGPA above 8, and prepare for service companies first while building projects for product firms.

Method

  • Loaded and checked the CSV with pandas (no duplicates, no merge needed).
  • Replaced placeholder salary 0 of unplaced students with missing values; standardised branch names.
  • Grouped and compared with pandas, charted with seaborn / matplotlib.
Dataset: Kaggle "Student Placement Details" (synthetic). Patterns may differ from real colleges.
